# GiftMoneyCalc — Design Context

## Users
20–40대 한국 직장인으로, 결혼·장례 등 경조사 참석이 빈번한 사람들을 대상으로 합니다. 축의금·부의금 액수를 정할 때마다 검색하고 지인에게 물어봐야 하는 반복적인 불편함을 겪고 있으며, 명확한 기준과 빠른 계산을 통해 그 불확실함을 해소하려는 사람들입니다.

## Brand Personality
**Pragmatic · Clear · Considerate**

Straightforward, non-judgmental guidance that respects social norms. Tone is efficient yet warm—user comes with uncertainty, receives confident, data-backed recommendations without making them feel anxious.

## Aesthetic Direction
TDS (Toss Design System) 준수. @toss/tds-mobile 컴포넌트 전용. 커스텀 미적 방향 없음.

## UX Principles
- **Minimize friction**: Quick input flows (relationship, intimacy level, event type) → instant result. Remove decision paralysis at the moment of need.
- **Transparency through data**: Show *why* the amount is recommended (relationship weight + regional factor + cost-of-living adjustment) so users gain confidence.
- **Build social proof**: History and statistics let users see what others in similar situations gave, turning isolation into community reference.
- **Enable future reuse**: Persistent history and downloadable reports make the calculator a recurring tool, not one-off lookup.
- **Respect time**: Mobile-first micro-interaction; results in <2 seconds; share/save actions instant.
